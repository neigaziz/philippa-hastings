# Fellowship assessment

Use for FLF, Discovery and related named research fellowships. Check the exact call: these schemes differ in remit, eligibility, duration, costs, career expectations and institutional nomination arrangements.

## Default weighted framework

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence to examine |
|---|---:|---|
| Research quality and independence | 40% | Originality and significance relative to career stage and opportunity; intellectual leadership in questions, design and interpretation; emerging identity and independence from supervisors; narrative assessment of outputs |
| Future vision and feasibility | 30% | Clear ambitious programme; original contribution; council remit and relevant priorities; methods, timeline, resources, risks, alternatives, collaborators and mentors |
| Leadership and service | 15% | Mentoring, teaching, peer review, organisation, public engagement and inclusive culture; leadership potential supported by contribution rather than title |
| Organisational fit and impact | 10% | Host strategy and facilities; specific commitments supporting independence; future development and funding; credible academic, societal or economic pathways where relevant |
| Communication and advocacy | 5% | Clear persuasive scientific narrative; supporting statements; interview advocacy and response to challenge when available |

Rate 0–5 using 0 = no demonstrated evidence, 1 = weak, 2 = partial, 3 = credible, 4 = strong, 5 = exceptional for career stage. Weighted total /100 = sum(weight × rating /5); divide by ten for /10. Label scoring illustrative and provisional where evidence is incomplete. Do not treat a missing host statement or unheld interview as a negative observed performance.

## Research programme examination

Identify the central problem, gap, hypothesis or exploratory rationale, aims and work packages. Test whether measurements and analyses address each aim. Examine sample-size justification, design, confounding, validation, ethics, reproducibility, data management, dependencies, costs and fallback approaches. Separate causal claims from associative evidence. Ask what is genuinely the applicant's contribution versus continuation of a supervisor's programme; collaboration is compatible with independence.

For host support, seek concrete access, protected time, mentorship, facilities, development opportunities and independence commitments rather than prestige alone. Match requirements to the call. Impact need not mean an immediate patent or commercial product; explain the plausible pathway and uncertainties.

## Optional rapid shortlist dashboard

For quick triage, report five separate 0–5 ratings: research track record; vision and feasibility; leadership and service; host environment; impact and communication. This is an unweighted diagnostic dashboard, not a second official rubric. Do not average it and present the result as the weighted score.

## Feedback priorities

Rank substantive scientific or eligibility issues before prose polishing. Distinguish fixable presentation gaps from missing preliminary evidence, unclear ownership, infeasible scope and inadequate host support. Suggest realistic actions and explain what evidence would change the judgement.
