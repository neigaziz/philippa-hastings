# Funding and programme verification

Keep current-rule verification proportionate to the request. No live verification is needed merely to improve sentence clarity. It is needed before asserting current eligibility, funding, scheme structures or exact programme features as confirmed.

## Evidence order

1. Exact current competition advert, applicant guidance and assessment criteria.
2. Current council/UKRI funding opportunity and applicable grant terms.
3. Current official programme or host admissions, funding and fee guidance.
4. Official historical guidance, explicitly dated and used only for historical questions.

Discover official pages with available search tools, then read the relevant page or document. Record page title, URL, call year/round, access date and relevant qualification. If sources conflict, identify the conflict and prioritise the exact call; recommend contacting the programme for unresolved details. User-provided official extracts can support provisional analysis, with provenance stated. Without browsing, mark current policy claims unverified rather than inventing citations.

## Claims requiring particular care

- International eligibility and allocations: the supplied profile's “typically 30%” is a historical/common rule of thumb to check, not a universal current ceiling or individual acceptance probability.
- Home/international fee status: nationality, residence, immigration status and fee assessment are distinct. Verify whether the award covers the international fee difference and additional expenses. Do not infer fee status from nationality alone.
- iCASE: check partner eligibility, co-funding, placement length, stipend enhancement, timing and award-specific obligations. The supplied three-month placement and enhanced stipend examples are not universal promises.
- Strategy: verify current terminology and distinguish UKRI-wide strategic themes from BBSRC-specific priorities. Treat “Rules of Life”, “Transformative Technologies”, “Better Health” and “Resilient World” as source-profile examples to contextualise, not an automatically current BBSRC list.
- Programme ecology: distinguish DTP, CTP, rotation, project studentship and fellowship by the actual scheme documents. “Responsive-mode studentship” is not a reliable universal scheme category without a source.
- Host resources: verify claims about Norwich Research Park facilities, Roslin resources, MIBTP computational training or any named laboratory. Do not invent access rights or assume facilities are available to the applicant.
- UKVI and grant terms: check the applicable current official guidance when relevant; assessment assistance is not an immigration or legal determination.

## Compact reporting

For each decisive rule: **Claim | Official source and date | Application to this candidate | Remaining uncertainty**. Keep this table short and relevant, not a generic policy lecture.
