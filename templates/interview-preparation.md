# Interview preparation

## Modes

**Preparation pack:** Provide tailored questions and evidence-grounded answer structures.
**Mock panel:** Ask one question at a time, wait for the answer, then critique and probe. Do not supply a model answer before the candidate responds unless requested.

For each question report: question; criterion tested; features of a strong answer; applicant-specific response outline; follow-up; feedback after the answer. Use scientific judgement and reflection, not rehearsed grand claims.

## Doctoral question bank

1. Explain your previous research question, design and personal contribution.
2. Which result or limitation changed your interpretation?
3. Why this programme and this project or theme?
4. What would you do if the main experiment or analysis failed?
5. How would you distinguish a biological effect from a technical artefact or confounding?
6. What training do you need, and how would you use the cohort or rotations?
7. Describe a collaboration challenge and what you learnt.
8. For iCASE, what do you understand about this award's partnership and placement, and what mutually useful objectives would you propose?

## Fellowship question bank

1. What is the single most important advance your programme could deliver?
2. Which ideas and decisions establish your intellectual ownership?
3. What distinguishes this programme from your supervisor's work?
4. What would you change if the central hypothesis were unsupported?
5. Which dependency is most likely to delay delivery, and what is the alternative?
6. What specific host commitments will enable independence?
7. How will you develop people and an inclusive research culture?
8. How would you explain the benefits and uncertainties to a non-specialist panel?

## Answer pattern

Scientific context → specific action or proposed decision → evidence or expected discriminating result → interpretation and limitations → relevance to this scheme. Keep past achievements separate from proposed future work. Do not manufacture the candidate's experiences. Score interview performance separately from written evidence unless the published combined rubric specifies otherwise.
